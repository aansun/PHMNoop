# Getting help with PHMN

PHMN is a personal project and an iOS app. It is a fork of [NOOP](https://github.com/ryanbr/noop); the strap protocol underneath is NOOP's.

- **Bugs and ideas → a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/aansun/PHMNoop/issues/new) on this repository.** In the app, **More › Feedback** (*Share feedback* or *Report a problem*) opens a new issue with its outline started and the app version, your iOS version, the model of your iPhone and the language filled in. Nothing about your health is added.
  For a problem with the strap, paste the **Strap log**: **More › Device Settings › Advanced › Strap log**, then the copy icon. It holds connection and sync events only, and without it a real fault is hard to tell from a setup detail.
- **Install and signing → [docs/IOS.md](docs/IOS.md) and [docs/SIGNING.md](docs/SIGNING.md).** The README covers installing, pairing a WHOOP 5.0 / MG, privacy and importing your history.
- **Questions about NOOP's own Mac or Android apps, or about the strap protocol, → [NOOP's issues and discussions](https://github.com/ryanbr/noop).** They are not part of PHMN and are not supported here.

I read every issue, but this is a project I work on for myself, so I cannot promise an answer or a fix.

PHMN is an independent, unofficial project, **not affiliated with WHOOP, Inc.**, and **not a medical device**.
