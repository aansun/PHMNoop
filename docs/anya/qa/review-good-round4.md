## Systemic patterns

- "Say you cannot tell" in the new contract is copied verbatim as "You cannot tell ..." (chat 2, 4, 5, 6, 13): wrong voice. It is the one new defect the contract rule wording created.
- The cause beat is still treated as mandatory: 21 of 25 answers name a cause for a good reading, even with the new rule. The phrase "likely" plus a vague cause ("consistent rest and recovery") is the model's default.
- "Keep today easy ... see whether it clears" is pasted onto green readings (Stress, HRV, Resp pages).
- Artefact strings become advice ("1 hour past what you need every night", "Strain 137.7 ... well above the range"). The model cannot tell an artefact from the user's data.
- Comparators are better but not solved: 22/9 as "against baseline", 82/54 as "yesterday's", eight values all labelled "today".
- Device and breathing profiles: a question she cannot answer from the status string gets the status pasted, not the general how-to; "hold" gets suggested in a breathing answer.
