## PHMN 3.0.1

Live heart rate back where you train, and an Anya that coaches the way the WHOOP app does.

### New / Enhancement
- **Anya, coach style.** Her instructions now start as a short, direct coach: the answer first, the figures behind it, one next step. Change or clear them under Anya's instructions.
- **Photos and files in the sheet.** The **+** in Anya's sheet adds a photo, a document or a lab file, as in the Anya tab.
- **Anya's name opens her settings.** Tap it at the top of the sheet. The Analyzing light now runs round the whole pill.
- **Recommended settings, offered once.** A neon card on Home the first time you open 3.0.1, and a short tour of the **+**: tap for shortcuts, hold to choose them.
- **5.0 / MG alarm on by default.** It was a switch under Experiments; it now starts on, so the alarm you set on Home buzzes.

### Bug Fixing
- **Live heart rate on a 5.0 / MG** now shows when you start a workout, during it and in the Health monitor, not only in Breathing.
- **Anya food estimates** no longer end in "couldn't read that as food" on the on-device model.
- **Let Anya use my numbers** lives in Anya settings only, not repeated on the connect pages.
- **Reports from the app** arrive with PHMN's own questions, not NOOP's.

### Install
`PHMN-ios-unsigned.ipa` is **unsigned** and has no Apple Watch app. Sign it with a sideloader (AltStore or SideStore), or yourself: see [docs/SIGNING.md](https://github.com/aansun/PHMNoop/blob/phm/docs/SIGNING.md).
With a free Apple ID, HealthKit is not available to a sideloaded build.

Everything the app shows is an estimate, not medical advice. PHMN is a fork of NOOP and independent of every brand it works with.
